## 七、给提示词工程师的硬性提醒

1. **外形逐字复用** `bible/characters.md` 的特征词，一字不改：纯白（`pure white`，不得写 creamy/beige）、圆头宽额、大圆眼宽眼距、短宽口鼻、大黑鼻、小三角耳、中等长度服帖毛、粉舌微露。
2. **耳朵必须写**：`small, neat triangular ears that sit close to the head, slightly folded forward at the tips`，并否定 `large erect pointed ears` / `big upright ears`。
3. **外八必须写**：`the front and hind paws turn slightly outward from the midline, a very subtle toe-out... not a wide bow-legged stance`。行走/站位镜头（04/12/17/20/21/30）全部带上。
